Performance comparison of SEANN and an agnostic DNN under increasing noise (Experiment 1). The x-axis shows the standard deviation of Gaussian noise added to all predictors. The y-axes report (top panel) predictive performance (R 2 ) and (bottom panel) the alignment of extracted dose–response relationships with the ground truth, quantified by Δ Shap (lower is better). Across noise levels, SEANN maintains higher predictive performance and more accurate relationships than the agnostic DNN.
