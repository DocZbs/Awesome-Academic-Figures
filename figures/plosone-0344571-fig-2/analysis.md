Visualization of microbial community distributions using PCA (left) and t-SNE (right). Each data point represents a microbial sample, colored by location. STR, MI, GLRC, and SUP correspond to the Mackinaw Straits, Lake Michigan, the Great Lakes Research Center at Michigan Tech, and Lake Superior, respectively.
