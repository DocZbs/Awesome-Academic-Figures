Corrected Ψ G versus standard quantum machine learning diagnostics at n = 6 in Regime B. Each panel plots the corrected Ψ G of Equation (1) audited against the natural target group of each ansatz on the vertical axis. The horizontal axes show the expressibility KL of [ 8 ] on the left, the gradient variance of [ 9 ] in the middle, and the Meyer-Wallach Q of [ 12 ] on the right. Under the PsiAudit component profile, they separate, primarily through the sectoral fluctuation M G rather than through the composite alone.
