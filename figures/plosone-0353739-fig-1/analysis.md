Sector-occupation trajectories at n = 6 in Regime B. Each row shows the sector-probability trajectory of one reference ansatz, audited against its natural target group. Rows from top to bottom show the hardware-efficient ansatz against U (1), the U (1)-equivariant ansatz against U (1), the SU (2)-equivariant ansatz against SU (2), the S n -equivariant ansatz against the S n Hamming-weight orbit decomposition, and the U (1)-broken ansatz against U (1). Within each row, columns index trajectory steps and rows index symmetry sectors. The colour scale shows sector probabilities from 0 to 1. Equivariant ansätze produce stable sectoral occupation patterns inherited from the product-state initialisation in Equation (7) , while the U (1)-broken ansatz develops a visibly drifting concentration.
