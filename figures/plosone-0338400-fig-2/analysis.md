Comparison of extracted relationships (Shapley values) between the agnostic DNN and SEANN (experiment 2). Only the beta coefficient for fish intake was added as external knowledge. SEANN not only recovers the expected protective effect of fish intake (close alignment with external knowledge), but also improves the capture of the correlated harmful effect of mercury exposure. By contrast, the agnostic DNN produces distorted or attenuated associations under the same noisy conditions.
