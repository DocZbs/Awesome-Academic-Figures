Feature selection. (A) LASSO cross-validation curve. The two dashed vertical lines indicate λmin, corresponding to the minimum cross-validation error, and λ1se, corresponding to the largest λ within one standard error of the minimum. The red dots represent the mean cross-validation error, the gray error bars indicate ±1 standard error, and the numbers above the curve denote the number of non-zero coefficients. (B) LASSO coefficient path showing the shrinkage trajectories of regression coefficients for different variables as a function of log(λ). (C) Changes in variable importance across repeated Boruta training iterations, used to evaluate the stability of variable importance and distinguish important variables from shadow features. (D) Box plot of the variable importance distribution generated by the Boruta algorithm. (E) Network diagram showing the overlapping and selected features identified by LASSO and Boruta.
